      SUM UP! rules

      The words that turn a message into a SUM UP! card without any AI, in English and Bahasa Indonesia. Click a word to switch it off, or add your own. Changes apply to new messages.

      Restricted words

      Words a number must never mention, each number with its own list. WA Turbo checks a message after you press Send, before it goes out. Spacing and punctuation don't matter, and captions and attachment names are checked; what is inside a photo is not. If a word looks close but not exact, the message and that word go to your connected AI to decide. With no AI connected, only exact words are caught.

      How to write your words
      • Add several at once. Separate them with commas. Capitals don't matter; words are kept in lowercase.
      • Add the short name and the full name. Enter them as separate words, because a name of several words only matches when all of it appears, in order.
      • Add a part people use by itself. If a long name is often shortened to one word, add that word too.
      • Skip ordinary words. Every match stops the message, so pick names that can only mean that company. Abbreviations that are also common terms will stop messages you meant to send.
      • Skip variants. Endings like -nya, -ku and -mu, spaces, dashes and dots inside a word, and capitals are already covered. Typos are too once a word has 3 letters and an AI is connected.
      • Set up every number. Each one has its own list. List the names of your other numbers, not its own.

      FAQ

      How WA Turbo behaves, in plain terms.

      Sent to the AIMessage text and the most recent photos, inside the window you choose.
      Never sent to the AIVoice notes, videos, documents, stickers and locations.
      Sent to WhatsAppOnly what you type or attach and send yourself.
      What does the AI actually read?

      Text and photos. When you summarize a chat or build a report, WA Turbo sends the message text inside the window you chose, plus up to the 10 most recent photos in that chat, so a transfer receipt or a screenshot of an error counts as part of the conversation. Photos the AI can see are tagged “Read by AI” in the chat.

      Everything else stays a placeholder. Voice notes, videos, documents, stickers and location pins are never sent; the model only sees that one was there. Turn photos off in the Model section above if you would rather send text only.

      How do restricted words work?

      Each number can have its own list in Settings → Restricted words. After you press Send or save an edit, WA Turbo looks for those words on this Mac, in the message and in the name of any attachment. An exact match holds the message back and tells you which word and which number; you can go back and fix it, or Send anyway. With Hard block on, the word has to come out first.

      If a word only looks close, such as a typo, the message and that word go to your connected AI, which answers with a list and nothing else. If the AI is slow or unavailable, the message still goes and a note tells you the AI check was skipped. The AI never writes or sends anything.

      Can I keep a photo from a chat?

      Yes. Every photo and file in a chat has a save button, and the full-size viewer has Save to this Mac. Opened photos are also kept in WA Turbo's own folder so they load instantly next time.

      Where do my messages go?

      Chats stay on this Mac, in the database built into WA Turbo. Only what you select for a summary or report is sent, and only to the AI you picked in Settings: Anthropic (Claude Code or an API key), Google (a Gemini key or Antigravity), OpenAI (Codex), DeepSeek or the service you entered. With "Local model" nothing leaves the Mac at all. There is no WA Turbo server in between. Phone numbers in the text are swapped for stable aliases before the request leaves the Mac. Photos are sent as they are, so a number or name inside a photo is not hidden. Generated reports are written locally and are not synced anywhere.

      Can WA Turbo send a message?

      Only when you send it. Type in the box under a chat, or attach a photo, and press Send or Enter. Nothing is ever sent on its own: no automatic replies, no scheduled messages, and the AI never writes or sends a message. WA Turbo also never sends read receipts or your online status. Nudge in SUM UP! only records that you followed up.

      How does SUM UP! find open loops?

      Simple rules run on this Mac as messages arrive: a question or request to you, your own ask, or your own promise. Nothing is sent anywhere to do that. Those loops carry an "auto" tag and a Not a loop button. When you summarize the chat, the AI reviews them and can dismiss false ones.

      The rules also close loops on their own: when you replied and the other side then said thanks, ok or noted, the loop closes and says why. The same question sent twice counts as one loop they chased.

      You can change the words these rules look for in Settings → SUM UP! rules.

      Does opening a chat clear an open loop?

      No, and that is deliberate. Read state and reply debt are separate: a loop closes when it is answered, never because you looked at the thread. That is the whole reason a chat you read on Monday still shows up in SUM UP! on Friday.

      Can one number see another number's chats?

      No. Each linked number keeps its own session and its own inbox, and there is no merged chat list. SUM UP! is the only place they are counted together, and every row there links back to the chat inside its own number.

      What restarts the waiting clock?

      A nudge. Total waiting time keeps counting from the original question, but the SLA clock restarts from your last follow-up — so "asked 4 days ago, chased an hour ago" and "asked 4 days ago, never chased" do not look the same.
