    FAQ

    How WA Turbo behaves, in plain terms.

    Sent to ClaudeMessage text and the most recent photos, inside the window you choose.
    Never sent to ClaudeVoice notes, videos, documents, stickers and locations.
    Sent to WhatsAppOnly what you type or attach and send yourself.
    What does the AI actually read?

    Text and photos. When you summarize a chat or build a report, WA Turbo sends the message text inside the window you chose, plus up to the 10 most recent photos in that chat, so a transfer receipt or a screenshot of an error counts as part of the conversation. Photos the AI can see are tagged “Read by AI” in the chat.

    Everything else stays a placeholder. Voice notes, videos, documents, stickers and location pins are never sent; the model only sees that one was there. Turn photos off in the Model section above if you would rather send text only.

    Can I keep a photo from a chat?

    Yes. Every photo and file in a chat has a save button, and the full-size viewer has Save to this Mac. Opened photos are also kept in WA Turbo's own folder so they load instantly next time.

    Where do my messages go?

    Chats stay on this Mac, in the database built into WA Turbo. Only what you select for a summary or report is sent, to Anthropic, either through the Claude Code app signed in on this Mac or with the API key you entered — there is no WA Turbo server in between. Phone numbers in the text are swapped for stable aliases before the request leaves the Mac. Photos are sent as they are, so a number or name inside a photo is not hidden. Generated reports are written locally and are not synced anywhere.

    Can WA Turbo send a message?

    Only when you send it. Type in the box under a chat, or attach a photo, and press Send or Enter. Nothing is ever sent on its own: no automatic replies, no scheduled messages, and Claude never writes or sends a message. WA Turbo also never sends read receipts or your online status. Nudge in SUM UP! only records that you followed up.

    How does SUM UP! find open loops?

    Simple rules run on this Mac as messages arrive: a question or request to you, your own ask, or your own promise. Nothing is sent anywhere to do that. Those loops carry an "auto" tag and a Not a loop button. When you summarize the chat, Claude reviews them and can dismiss false ones.

    The rules also close loops on their own: when you replied and the other side then said thanks, ok or noted, the loop closes and says why. The same question sent twice counts as one loop they chased.

    Does opening a chat clear an open loop?

    No, and that is deliberate. Read state and reply debt are separate: a loop closes when it is answered, never because you looked at the thread. That is the whole reason a chat you read on Monday still shows up in SUM UP! on Friday.

    Can one number see another number's chats?

    No. Each linked number keeps its own session and its own inbox, and there is no merged chat list. SUM UP! is the only place they are counted together, and every row there links back to the chat inside its own number.

    What restarts the waiting clock?

    A nudge. Total waiting time keeps counting from the original question, but the SLA clock restarts from your last follow-up — so "asked 4 days ago, chased an hour ago" and "asked 4 days ago, never chased" do not look the same.
